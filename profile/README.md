## What we do

Intake answers your customers using your own knowledge: your articles, your documents, and the way your team works. When a case needs a person, it hands it to your team with the full context.

- 🤖 **[AI agent](https://www.intake.es/en/product/ai-agent)**: answers from your knowledge base and [takes action](https://www.intake.es/en/product/capabilities/run-actions), not just replies.
- 📥 **[Inbox](https://www.intake.es/en/product/inbox)**: every conversation in one place, whatever the channel.
- 🙋 **[Human handoff](https://www.intake.es/en/product/human-handoff)**: the agent knows when to stop and who to bring in.
- 📚 **[Help center](https://www.intake.es/en/product/help-center)**: spots [repeated questions](https://www.intake.es/en/product/capabilities/detect-repeated-questions) and [suggests new articles](https://www.intake.es/en/product/capabilities/suggest-new-articles).
- 📊 **[Insights](https://www.intake.es/en/product/insights)**: what your customers ask and how it's getting resolved.

## Where it works

Chat on your website, WhatsApp, and Slack, connected to the tools you already use: HubSpot, Notion, Jira, Shopify, Google Drive, Zapier, Make, and [more integrations](https://www.intake.es/en/product/integrations).
